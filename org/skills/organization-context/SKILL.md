# Organization context

Use the organization-owned material under `/org/workspace` when it is relevant.
Never modify `/org`; personal artifacts belong in the tenant workspace.

## Naming (strict)

The firm is **BugleRock** — one word, capital B and capital R, no space, no
umlaut, never in all caps. Write it that way in every document, deck, email and
message. The umlaut belongs to the logo artwork only and must never appear in
text, including body copy, slide titles and footers.

Entities, when one must be named in full:

- **BugleRock Holdings Pte. Ltd. (BRHPL)** — investment banking.
- **BugleRock Capital Pte. Ltd. (BRCPL)** — wealth and asset management,
  Singapore, and BugleRock Capital in India.

The tagline is **Sound of Clarity**.

## Documents

Client-facing documents default to a white background and use the firm's
palette: Berry `#912F63` and Deep Plum `#3E3452` as primaries.

Do not put a client's name alongside their holdings, AUM or returns in any
document unless it is addressed to that client. Keep live mandates,
counterparties and valuations out of anything that is not explicitly internal.
